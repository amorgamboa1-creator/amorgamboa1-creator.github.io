LAUNCH WITHOUT A THIRD-PARTY WEBSITE BUILDER
1. Keep your domain (for example, gacreativeagency.com.au) pointed to your own web hosting.
2. Open your hosting File Manager or FTP.
3. Open the domain's document root (often public_html).
4. Upload index.html and style.css. Upload ga-logo.png if you want to use the supplied logo image.
5. Open your domain in a browser.

IMPORTANT
- The page is custom HTML/CSS; no Shopify, Wix, Squarespace or page builder is required.
- The form currently uses mailto as a temporary no-third-party solution. A server-side PHP form can be added later if your hosting supports PHP.
- The example phone number and email address in the page are placeholders. Replace them before publishing.
- The package prices are starting/test prices and should be reviewed before going live.
- The hero/portfolio backgrounds are CSS placeholders. Replace them with your licensed property images when ready.
